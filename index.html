Who we are

First Horse is a registered company specialised in the supply of active pharmaceutical ingredients, excipients, vitamins, herbal extracts, flavours, cosmetic and food ingredients, oils, probiotics and enzymes. We are also a registered agent for medical packaging — injection pens, vaccine bottles, animal drug packaging, caps, bottles and aluminium closures — as well as laboratory reagents, standards and pharmaceutical impurities.
