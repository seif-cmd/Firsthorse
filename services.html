Our services

Everything between the raw ingredient and the compliant shipment.

Export solution services

We study global markets to find where an export makes sense, then match exporters with the right importers — and importers with the right suppliers.
  • Analysis of global markets for export
  • Matching exporters with appropriate global partners (and importers with the right suppliers)
  • Strategy support for sales growth and competitiveness
  • Documentation support for exporters and importers with all regulatory parties
  • Ongoing monitoring of the export process and business development
Sourcing

Packaging

Exclusive packaging

We're proud to be the exclusive Middle East representative of a leading pharmaceutical packaging company. That specialisation brings unparalleled expertise, innovation and a commitment to excellence to every project we take on.
Logistics

Import for other companies

First Horse holds official licenses to import and export goods on behalf of other companies. We provide comprehensive logistical support and ensure smooth, efficient, cross-border operations that are compliant at every step — backed by a deep understanding of international trade regulations.
Machinery

Pharmaceutical machine services

We cover the machinery required across tableting and powder, liquid oral, ointment, and injectable production — supported by professionally qualified engineers with in-house CAD design tools and production support software. Our partner factories run their own machine shop, fabrication shop, assembly section, and quality assurance department, equipped to the latest testing standards required for U.S. FDA, MCA and WHO approval. Round-the-clock after-sales support is provided by a dedicated team of qualified engineers.
Regulatory

Registration services

We prepare the documentation pharmaceutical products need to reach the market compliantly.
  • EDA registration
  • NAFSA registration
  • CTD file preparation
  • Cost sheet preparation
  • Scientific file preparation (composition, mode of action, and more)
  • Pamphlet preparation
  • Export and import permissions
